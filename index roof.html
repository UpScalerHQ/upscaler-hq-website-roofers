Common Questions

Straight Answers.

I've tried roofing leads before and got nothing but tyre kickers.

Shared lead sellers send the same enquiry to four roofers and let you fight over it. That's not what this is. These are your leads, exclusively, qualified on ownership, roof type and timeframe before they reach your phone — and followed up within minutes instead of hours.

Competition confirms the demand is there. The roofers losing work in busy markets are the ones taking six hours to return a call and never chasing a quote. When you respond first, qualify properly and follow up consistently, you pick up the jobs your competitors let go cold.

I'm on the tools all day. I don't have time for another platform.

The system runs without you. Leads are captured and qualified automatically, follow-up is automated, and the CRM manages the pipeline. Your job is turning up to the inspection and doing the work. We handle the rest.
